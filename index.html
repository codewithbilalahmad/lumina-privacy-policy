Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

Welcome to Lumina - Photo Editing. Your privacy is important to us. Lumina does not require you to create an account and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or home address.

📷 Camera Permission

Used only when you choose to capture a photo inside the app.

🖼 Photos & Videos Permission

Required to select images from your device and save edited photos.

🔔 Notifications (Android 13+)

Used to provide app notifications when enabled.

2. How We Use Information

  • Edit your photos.
  • Apply filters and effects.
  • Crop, rotate and resize images.
  • Save edited photos.
  • Share photos using Android's share feature.

All editing is performed locally on your device whenever possible.

3. Advertising

Lumina may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect limited information such as:

  • Advertising ID
  • Device identifiers
  • App interactions
  • Approximate location (provided by Google services)

Learn more:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Third-Party Services

Lumina may use trusted third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Firebase (if enabled)

5. Buy Me a Coffee

Lumina includes a Buy Me a Coffee option for users who wish to support development.

Payments are securely processed by Buy Me a Coffee. Lumina never stores your payment information.

6. Data Storage

Edited photos remain on your device. Lumina does not upload your images to our servers.

7. Sharing

Photos are shared only when you explicitly choose to share them.

8. Data Security

Your photos remain under your control. We take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Children's Privacy

Lumina does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page.
