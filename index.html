Information We Collect

Welcome to Lumina – Photo Editing. Your privacy is at the heart of our design. Lumina does not require an account, and we never collect personal data like your name, email, address, or phone number.

🖼 Photos & Videos

Required to choose images from your device and to save your beautiful edits locally.

How We Use Information

  • Edit, crop, rotate, and resize your photos
  • Apply filters, effects, and adjustments
  • Save your edited images directly to your device
  • Share photos via Android’s native share sheet
⚡ All processing happens locally — your images never leave your device.

Advertising

Lumina shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the app free.

Third‑Party Services

  • Google AdMob

Data Storage

All edited photos are stored only on your device. Lumina does not upload, backup, or transfer your images to any external server.

Sharing

Photos are shared only when you explicitly choose the share option. We never initiate sharing automatically.

Data Security

Your photos remain under your control. We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure — we encourage you to keep your device updated.

Children's Privacy

Lumina does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us.
